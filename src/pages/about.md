## About Therapeutic Window

In pharmacology, the therapeutic window is the range between a dose that works and a dose that does harm. Most of biotech lives inside that kind of tension: promising science against clinical risk, big rounds against long timelines, hype against data.

This newsletter covers the news that matters in that space: funding and deals, clinical and FDA updates, and the science behind them. I try to explain what happened, why it matters, and what I'd be watching next. I aim to do that without the jargon or the spin.

## About me

I'm Mohammed Hazari. I work in operations at Angel Star Ventures, a venture group investing in biotech and healthtech, where I lead due diligence and investor relations.

Before venture, I worked in the lab. I did metabolic research at UT Southwestern's Touchstone Diabetes Center, which led to a publication in *JCI Insight*, and I later worked with Fauna Bio and Solaxa on the company side. I have an MS in biomedical sciences from Rutgers and a BS from UT Dallas.

I read biotech news the way I've learned to read a pitch deck: start with the data, then look at the story.
